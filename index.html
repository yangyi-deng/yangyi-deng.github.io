About

Hi, welcome to my website! I am a PhD candidate in Business Economics at the Department of Decisions, Operations and Technology, The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) Business School.

My research studies how economic measures and rankings should be designed when the objects being measured are multidimensional, interdependent, or imperfectly observed — features that standard measures often fail to capture. I identify the principles a well-developed measure should satisfy, characterize the aggregation rules implied by those principles, and evaluate their empirical implications. Applications include scholarly evaluation, market concentration and merger screening, and ESG ratings. I also study how artificial intelligence shapes incentives, cooperation, and the organization of teamwork.
